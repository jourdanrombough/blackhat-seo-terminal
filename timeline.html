A selective chronology of documented announcements, enforcement accounts and research. Each row links to its source and states what the event can establish. Publication, effective and observed dates are distinguished where they differ. This is not a causal history of every ranking change or a measurement of when a technique became popular.

WHAT A TIMELINE DOES NOT PROVE

A loss near an update is temporal association. Establishing causation requires evidence about the affected pages, concurrent deployments, measurement, comparison cases and alternative explanations. Older unsourced claims about Florida, “Medic,” the birth of PBNs and universal tactic decline have been removed from this timeline. Named incidents and unresolved outcomes remain in the case database, where their evidence can be assessed separately.

About this archive

Educational research on search and AI manipulation. Consult the methodology, source register and claim-specific limitations. Vendor claims, proposed studies and observed local demonstrations are different evidence categories.

Research status

Evidence cutoff: 2026-09-19. Editorial revision: 2026-09-19. Local fixtures demonstrate mechanisms, not Google ranking efficacy. The review tracker records remaining limitations; the build date does not verify claims.

Scope

Use demonstrations on owned or isolated test infrastructure. Policy, contractual, civil and criminal consequences depend on the conduct and applicable rules; they are not interchangeable labels.
