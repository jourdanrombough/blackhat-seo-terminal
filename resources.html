RESOURCES & REFERENCES

A source map for defensive investigation and historical research. Begin with the applicable policy or documented incident, then use logs and independent evidence to assess a specific claim. Community discussion can suggest questions; it does not by itself establish effectiveness, prevalence or legality.

EDUCATIONAL RESOURCES

Forums and communities

Earlier editions listed BlackHatWorld (also abbreviated BHW), Warrior Forum, WickedFire and AffiliateFix alongside precise membership counts. The counts were unsupported and have been removed; BHW is not a separate community from BlackHatWorld. These names are retained as historical research leads, not as a verified list of currently active marketplaces.

Five named Telegram channels and their audience counts were also unverified. They are not carried forward as recommendations. A handle may be renamed, impersonated or transferred, and a subscriber total does not count unique operators, customers or transactions. This edition makes no current channel-activity or market-size claim.

How to evaluate a community claim

  1. Record the exact public URL, author identifier, observation time and any edits. Distinguish the poster’s claim from your observation.
  2. Establish the denominator: all eligible campaigns, only volunteered success stories, or merely visible posts. Deleted failures and vendor promotion create selection bias.
  3. Seek independently inspectable measurements with dates, controls and disclosed incentives. A screenshot can omit query context, localization, unsuccessful tests or paid traffic.
  4. Report unresolved identity and access limits. Do not infer current operation from an old thread or assume an inaccessible page has closed.

Vocabulary

The canonical glossary defines core and extended terms in one place. In particular, cloaking includes deceptive intent; nofollow is a hint rather than a binary transfer switch; prompt injection can be visible text; and SEO poisoning can use attacker-owned as well as compromised sites.

DEFENSIVE REFERENCE LIBRARY

Google policy and diagnostic sources

These are first-party descriptions of Google’s policies and public systems, not independent measurements of every enforcement outcome. Separate a documented rule from a proposed explanation of a rank change.

SourceUse and limits
Search EssentialsTechnical and content eligibility principles; meeting them does not guarantee indexing.
Spam policiesDefinitions of manipulative practices. Match the documented conduct to the policy rather than inferring a violation from a tool name.
Site reputation policy: August 2026 updateCurrent editorial-integration and EEA/outside-EEA distinctions; preserve historical dates when describing older actions.
Ranking systems guideSystem descriptions and retired systems, including the March 2024 helpful-content integration.
Manual Actions reportConfirm the specific manual action and required remediation; a traffic drop alone is not a manual action.
Disavow guidanceConsiderable artificial/low-quality links plus actual or likely manual action are the relevant conditions; most sites do not need this tool.
Local ranking guidanceRelevance, distance and prominence; third-party local metrics are not substitutes for Google’s stated factors.
Structured data guidelinesEligibility and content-consistency requirements; valid markup is not a ranking guarantee.
Search documentation updatesCurrent feature removals and documentation changes. FAQ rich results ended May 7, 2026.
HowTo / FAQ feature changesHistorical feature changes; HowTo rich results ended September 13, 2023.
Crawler request verificationHow site operators verify crawler requests; not evidence of Google’s undisclosed cloaking detector.
Search status dashboardOfficial ranking, indexing and serving incidents; incident timing alone does not prove the cause of one site’s losses.

Defensive monitoring toolkit

Evidence classExamples / roleWhat it cannot establish alone
First-party site telemetrySearch Console, analytics and access logs: record affected URLs, queries, timestamps and server responses.Search Console reporting is scoped and aggregated; analytics changes may reflect consent, tagging or channel changes.
Third-party crawl/link toolsAhrefs, Semrush and site crawlers: discover patterns and compare snapshots. See the individual catalogue records.Their crawls are samples; an authority score or backlink spike does not prove a Google action.
Content comparisonText-diff and similarity tools: locate copied passages, hidden elements and template changes.Similarity does not prove authorship, legal infringement or AI generation. AI detectors are not conclusive attribution evidence.
Historical capturesThe Internet Archive can document captured versions and redirects.A capture may omit scripts or personalization. Missing snapshots are not proof a page never existed.
Security investigationSite integrity records, endpoint telemetry and threat reports: connect landing pages, redirects, downloads and execution.A scanner verdict is one signal. Preserve evidence and assess the actual behavior and observation time.

No price ranges are repeated here. Commercial offerings, free access limits and supported environments require product-specific checks; see the dated catalogue evidence notes.

Legal and regulatory sources

Policy breach, contractual breach, civil liability and a criminal offence are different conclusions. A technical description or Google policy cannot substitute for the elements of a statute or a court record.

AuthorityRelevant distinction
FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials RuleEffective October 21, 2024; specified fake-review practices and sentiment-conditioned incentives, with civil penalties for knowing violations.
FTC Endorsement GuidesMaterial connections and endorsement disclosures are separate from assuming that every endorsement falls under the 2024 rule.
UK fake-review commencementFake-review provisions took effect April 6, 2025; distinguish the Act’s name/year from commencement.
CMA unfair-commercial-practices guidanceParagraph 13 and footnote 39 explain the fake-review prohibition and its exclusion from the automatic criminal offences for most other banned practices.
DOJ: William Stanley sentencingA documented extortion case. Its charge and sentence are not a rule that all negative SEO is the same offence.

Other conduct may raise computer-access, privacy, fraud, defamation or copyright issues. The legal issue map is a starting point for identifying questions, not a finding that any named practice meets a statute’s elements.

Industry reporting and research

Google announcements establish its public position; regulator and court materials establish the action they describe; research papers establish results only within their studied samples and methods. Trade press can document events and statements. Practitioner posts are useful leads but need controls, independent corroboration and disclosure of commercial interests before generalization.

The bibliography separates these source types and records citation use and access limitations. The timeline maintains event dates; the glossary maintains terminology; the appendix contains reading paths, evidence questions and the shell manual. Rebuilding the site does not reverify external material.

About this archive

Educational research on search and AI manipulation. Consult the methodology, source register and claim-specific limitations. Vendor claims, proposed studies and observed local demonstrations are different evidence categories.

Research status

Evidence cutoff: 2026-09-19. Editorial revision: 2026-09-19. Local fixtures demonstrate mechanisms, not Google ranking efficacy. The review tracker records remaining limitations; the build date does not verify claims.

Scope

Use demonstrations on owned or isolated test infrastructure. Policy, contractual, civil and criminal consequences depend on the conduct and applicable rules; they are not interchangeable labels.
