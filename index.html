READING THIS EDITION

Read the completed archive-search study and reproduce its 45 paired observations. The six offline demonstrations use synthetic fixtures; four proposed external studies have no reported results. Start with Methodology and the review status.

START HERE

Explore the mechanisms, history and defensive analysis of search and AI manipulation. This edition integrates source-backed audit corrections and supplies reproducible offline demonstrations plus one measured local search evaluation. Proposed ranking and citation studies remain unexecuted; chapter-specific evidence limits are part of the reading, not a claim of thesis completion.

Read each evidence label together with its linked source and limitations. A label alone does not establish accuracy; unverified fields and unexecuted protocols remain explicit.

About this archive

Educational research on search and AI manipulation. Consult the methodology, source register and claim-specific limitations. Vendor claims, proposed studies and observed local demonstrations are different evidence categories.

Research status

Evidence cutoff: 2026-09-19. Editorial revision: 2026-09-19. Local fixtures demonstrate mechanisms, not Google ranking efficacy. The review tracker records remaining limitations; the build date does not verify claims.

Scope

Use demonstrations on owned or isolated test infrastructure. Policy, contractual, civil and criminal consequences depend on the conduct and applicable rules; they are not interchangeable labels.
