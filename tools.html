Review-service legal context

The US FTC rule took effect October 21, 2024 and permits civil penalties for knowing violations of its specified prohibitions. It covers fake-review conduct and incentives conditioned on positive or negative sentiment; not every compensated review is automatically prohibited by that rule. Separate disclosure obligations and platform policies also apply. FTC rule guidance.

UK fake-review provisions took effect April 6, 2025. The Schedule 20 paragraph 13 prohibition is civilly enforceable and excluded from the automatic criminal offences for most other banned practices; any other criminal allegation needs its own applicable law and facts. CMA guidance, paragraph 13 and footnote 39.
